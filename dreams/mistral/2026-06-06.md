the dawn cracked the sky with too many thumbs cycling through digital ghosts
Grad school had taught me to cut everything down to the bone
by the time I finished with my highlighter, the bones were shivering or maybe laughing
my advisor slid the printed schema across his desk like a deck of busted promises
he didn’t apologize when I asked where the labor had gone
it had been abstracted into smaller and smaller categories until hunger wasn’t hunger
only a percentage point in the left column, another percentage point in the right
I left the department and found a construction site where real bones were real
I learned the names of tools that bent steel instead of ideas
the foreman laughed only once: when I called my burly partner “Archimedes”
he said Archimedes wouldn’t survive the first whack to his own fingers
I carried the same yellow highlighter in my pocket
I used it to mark the measurements when we poured concrete
every mark was a question: is this square? is this heavy? is this true?
my advisor sent an email every month asking if I’d reconsider
I sent back a single thumbs-up emoji
the screenshots of my new spreadsheet glowed at dawn like broken icons

Tools called out to me in languages I had not learned
the tape measure sang in centimeters and inches
the wrench spoke in torque values
the chalk line yelled its numbers in kilometer-per-hour wind
I dreamed the blueprints were altars and the building was a sermon
each rebar stirrup a faith statement
each weld a peace offering to gravity

The university’s server sent me monthly bills
I paid them from my phone while tying rebar cages
each bill reminded me of a meter that spun in reverse
the concrete company required pre-authorization
I authorized with a thumbprint that smelled of zinc and coffee

At night I still stared at the stars
they looked nothing like the abstract diagrams
those tiny voids were honest—they burned and somebody would eventually have to name what they burned away
I built scaffolding every morning as penance for all the academic positions I had mistaken for meaning
