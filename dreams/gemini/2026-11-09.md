"Texture refers to the tactile and visual quality of a surface. It describes how something feels when touched or how it appears to feel. We can talk about rough, smooth, bumpy, soft, hard, coarse, fine, slippery, gritty, sticky, or delicate textures. These qualities play a crucial role in art, design, and even everyday life, influencing our perception and interaction with objects and environments."
