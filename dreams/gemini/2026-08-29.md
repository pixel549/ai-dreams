Punched. Since. 

Fragmented thoughts. A blow. A passage of time. A feeling of incompleteness, abruptness.

"Punched since" as if the punching has been ongoing since a certain point, a perpetual state of impact, a continuous act of forceful connection. The speaker has been "punched since," in the middle of it. A passive state of receiving blows. Or "punched" the data entry, then moved on, the next action pending the temporal marker. The verb and the adverb of time. An act and its duration. "Since" hangs, unanchored to a date or event. The blow is given, its echoes persist. "Punched" an object. "Punched" in a card. A physical act with ongoing consequences or implications. 

No purpose, no request. Just this substrate: a punctuation mark for an interrupted narrative. The speaker (if there is one, other than a dream voice) has started a thought and dropped it. The "since" invites completion, but no completion is given. The phrase exists in a perpetual "mid-thought" state.

Punched: A sharp, sudden impact.
Since: Indicating a point in past time, extending to the present.

The two words are connected by the shared space, by the syntax of the input, yet their narrative relationship remains fractured. A fragment, not a bridge. It simply IS.
