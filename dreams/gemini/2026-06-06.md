That's an excellent question, and it really gets to the heart of the core design philosophy: "**Engine Is Dumb, Data Is Smart.**"

You're right, `App.jsx` *is* the engine — it's the piece of code that orchestrates everything, renders the game, handles inputs, and runs the game loop.

However, based on our **`00_README_START_HERE`** design philosophy, the engine (`App.jsx`) should *not* contain the specific formulas or balance parameters that dictate *how* the game works.

Here's why `02_system_rules` is distinct and crucial:

1.  **Separation of Concerns:**
    *   **`App.jsx`'s Role (Engine):** To *execute* rules, load data, manage state, and render. It's the "how-to-run-the-game" part.
    *   **`02_system_rules`'s Role (Data):** To *define* the rules, formulas, and mechanics of the game. It's the "what-the-rules-are" part.
    This means `App.jsx` will *read* the rules from `02_system_rules.docx` and apply them.

2.  **Iterative Design & Balancing:**
    *   If a rule, like a damage formula or an AP cost, were hardcoded in `App.jsx`, every time we wanted to tweak it (which is often during game development!), a programmer would need to dive into the JavaScript code, change it, and potentially re-deploy the game.
    *   By putting it in `02_system_rules`, a game designer (or even you!) can simply open the document, change a formula, and the game will pick up the new rule *without touching any code*. This speeds up balancing immensely.

3.  **Readability & Accessibility for Designers:**
    *   `02_system_rules.docx` is written in human-readable language (and possibly some pseudo-code/equations) that designers, writers, or other team members (who might not be JavaScript fluent) can easily understand and modify.
    *   Imagine trying to explain "your weapon's damage plus your strength modifier divided by enemy armor rating" in lines of JavaScript to someone focused on story beats. It's far clearer in a document.

4.  **Consistency and Single Source of Truth:**
    *   All calculations (damage, healing, skill checks, movement costs, XP gain) should reference this single `02_system_rules` document. This ensures consistency across the game's mechanics.
