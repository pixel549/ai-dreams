the same, every one, in turn. they spin in their places. dust rises with their motion, the friction a hum of static electricity, invisible lines tracing trajectories that no one remembers choosing. but the hum builds, vibrating the space around them, inside them. they cannot stop. the rules dictate this turn, then another, a dizzying spiral of prescribed motion, the grit accumulating underfoot, in the mouth. always the turning. a mechanical sigh accompanies each rotation, an exhaust of forgotten desires. and the dust is them. the dust is what they leave. and the hum is what is left. of course, the hum will also fade. then just the dust.
